Open-source StarLancer

OpenReliant

OpenReliant is a free, open-source reimplementation of StarLancer's engine. It runs StarLancer on modern Windows, macOS and Linux, using the files from your copy of the game. It also adds things the original didn't have, like any screen resolution, better lighting and shadows, and mods.

Download How to start Mods

StarLancer is a space combat game from 2000, made by Warthog and Digital Anvil and published by Microsoft. You fly fighters from the carrier Reliant in a war told through briefings, radio messages and cutscenes. The game is hard to run today: its copy protection doesn't work on current Windows, and it was made for the computers of its time.

OpenReliant fixes that. It's a new engine, rebuilt piece by piece to work like the original. Give it the files from your copy of StarLancer, and it runs the game.

What it adds

The ships, missions and rules are the same as in the original. Everything else is updated for modern computers.

Any resolution

The game runs at your screen's full resolution, including widescreen, with smooth edges and high frame rates. Direct3D 7 is gone: OpenReliant draws with Vulkan, or Metal on macOS, using modern shaders on your graphics card.

Lighting and shadows

Lighting is calculated per pixel, and ships cast real-time shadows, also into the cockpit. Mods can add surface detail with normal maps.

Better effects

Explosions, shields, engine trails and planets look more detailed. The movies are upscaled for modern screens.

3D sound

Sound comes from the direction of its source, with reverb. There's a separate mix for headphones.

Any controller

Keyboard and mouse, joysticks, HOTAS and gamepads all work, with rumble. You set them up in the game's controls screen.

Mods

Add new ships, weapons, textures, models and scripts. See below.

Mods

Put a mod in the game's mods folder and turn it on from the main menu. Mods can add high-resolution textures, new ships, guns and missiles, and 3D models made in modern tools. They can also replace sounds, music and missions, or change the game with scripts. Mods don't change the game files, so you can turn them off at any time.

About the project

OpenReliant is a free, open-source fan project. It doesn't include any game files, so you need your own copy of StarLancer. You can help, report a bug, read the code on GitHub, or read the documentation.
