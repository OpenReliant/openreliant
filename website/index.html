What's different

OpenReliant plays StarLancer the way it was made: the same ships, missions, wingmen and rules. What changes is everything between the game and you, so it feels at home on a modern computer.

Sharper and smoother

The game fills today's wide screens at their full resolution and runs smoothly at high frame rates. Ships catch the light and cast shadows, and explosions, shields and planets have more to them than they used to.

Sound all around you

Every engine, shot and explosion comes from where it is, the cockpit has its own echo, and headphones get a mix of their own.

Fly with what you have

Keyboard and mouse, a flight stick, a full HOTAS setup or a gamepad, with rumble for every hit, set up from the game's own controls screen.

True to the original

OpenReliant rebuilds the game's own engine piece by piece, so the ships fly, fight and talk the way they always did. Where the original had a clear bug, it's fixed; where it made a choice, the choice stays.

Mods, built in

Drop a mod into the game's mods folder and turn it on from the main menu. Mods can sharpen the game with high-resolution textures, add new ships, guns and missiles, bring models from today's 3D tools, replace sounds, music and missions, and change how the game plays with scripts. Nothing of the game's own files is touched, so turning a mod off brings the game back as it was.

The game

StarLancer is a space combat game by Warthog and Digital Anvil, published by Microsoft in 2000. You join a squadron of volunteers flying from the carrier Reliant, through a campaign of missions with wingmen, briefings and cinematics.

The project

OpenReliant is a free, open-source fan project, made by people who love the game. It contains none of the game's files, so you need your own copy. Anyone can help out, report a bug, or read the code.
