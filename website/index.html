The game

StarLancer is a space combat simulator by Warthog and Digital Anvil, published by Microsoft in 2000. You fly fighters from the carrier Reliant through a campaign of missions, with a full cast of wingmen, briefings and cinematics.

OpenReliant isn't affiliated with any of them. It needs the game's files from a copy you own, and holds none of them.

The engine

OpenReliant reimplements the original engine function by function from the original executable, in Zig on SDL3, and draws with Vulkan, or Metal on macOS. It plays the game as the original does by default.

Where it improves on the original, such as widescreen or per-pixel lighting, --original brings back the original's look and sound.

What works

The first campaign mission plays from start to finish. The rest of the campaign and multiplayer are in progress.

Flight and combat

Every ship, with the original flight model, weapons, damage, AI and cockpit displays, and eight camera views including 3D cockpits.

Missions

The game's missions and custom ones load as the original loads them. Mission 1's script runs in full; the commands later missions use are coming.

Graphics

Native resolution in 32-bit colour, per-pixel lighting, real-time shadows, bloom, anti-aliasing, smooth motion at high frame rates, and richer explosions, shields and planets.

Sound

3D positional sound with reverb, and HRTF for headphones.

Controls

Mouse and keyboard, flight sticks, HOTAS and gamepads, force feedback as rumble, and the bindings of the game's controls screen.

Front end

The main menu, settings, pilot roster and saved games, and the Reliant's rooms: briefings, the loadout, the news and the ITAC's debriefings.

Mods

Replace or add any game file, PNG textures of any size with normal, material and glow maps, new ships, guns and missiles, models from OBJ and glTF, and Luau scripts. Browse the mods.

Tools

sltool comes with every release: it inspects, exports and packs the game's formats, from archives and models to missions and sound banks.

The roadmap

Make mods

Mods are folders or .hog archives in the game's mods folder, described by a mod.ini. The guides cover textures, models, ship types and scripts, and the mods site has mods checked to work and to be free to share.
